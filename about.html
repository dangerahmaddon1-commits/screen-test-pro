About Screen Test Pro

Screen Test Pro is a simple browser-based visual aid for inspecting displays with solid colours and an alignment grid. It is designed for people who want to look for possible pixel defects, uneven brightness, or unusual display patterns without installing software.

How it works

The tool changes the page background to a selected colour or grid. It does not access hardware diagnostic interfaces, automatically detect defects, calibrate the screen, or repair a display.

Accuracy and independence

What you see depends on your display technology, brightness, browser, viewing angle, and room lighting. Screen Test Pro is an independent project and is not affiliated with Dell, HP, Lenovo, Samsung, LG, Apple, or other display manufacturers.
