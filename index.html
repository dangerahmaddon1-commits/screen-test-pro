How to Test Your LCD Monitor & Laptop Screen for Dead Pixels

A dead pixel remains permanently unlit and appears as a tiny black dot on solid color backgrounds. A stuck pixel stays illuminated in a single RGB color (red, green, or blue). By cycling through fullscreen primary colors, you can inspect any Dell, HP, Lenovo, or local/Chinese LCD panel for panel defects.

Checking Black Screen Backlight Bleed & Touch Screen Uniformity

Backlight bleeding occurs when light leaks from the edges of an LCD panel, which is most visible on a full Black Screen in dim lighting. Our online touch screen test tool also helps verify response uniformities across square and widescreen displays.
